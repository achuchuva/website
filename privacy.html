Privacy Policy

Applies to all games and applications published by Anton Chuchuva.

1. Data Collection

Anton Chuchuva ("I", "me", or "my") develops and publishes video games across mobile, desktop, and web platforms. I do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal or sensitive user data (such as your name, email address, location, or device identifiers).

2. Third-Party Services and Game Engines

My games are built using third-party game engines (such as Unity) and distributed through digital storefronts (such as Google Play, Steam, and itch.io). While I do not personally collect your data, these underlying platforms and engines may automatically process limited, non-personal technical data—such as device type, operating system version, and crash logs—to ensure app stability and security.

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy

3. Children’s Privacy

Because my games do not collect personal information from any users, they do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.

4. Changes to This Policy

If future games introduce online features, leaderboards, analytics, or advertising, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly prior to release.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via my Contact page.
